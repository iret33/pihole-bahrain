# Name and logo policy

The code of Sinko is free software under the GPL (see [`COPYING.md`](COPYING.md)). **The name "Sinko", the Arabic
spelling سينكو used as a product name, and the Sinko logo and wordmarks are the project's marks.** The GPL gives you
the code, not the marks. This policy is meant to keep it clear to buyers and users who made what they hold.

## You may, without asking

* Say that you use Sinko, review it, teach it, write about it, or that your product is **"compatible with" or "built
  on" Sinko**, as long as it is true.
* Use the name and logo in a link to this project, in screenshots of the page, and in tutorials.
* Build, share and install your own copy for your own family or for friends, under the name Sinko, unmodified.
* Sell help, support or installation services for Sinko, if you say plainly that you are independent.

## Please ask first

* Selling **ready-made boxes or flashed cards under the Sinko name**. Official Sinko boxes are the ones sold by the
  project maintainer and the resellers the maintainer names on the project website. If you want to resell them, or sell
  your own flashed units under the Sinko name, ask for written permission.
* Using the logo on packaging, shop listings or a website that could look like the official one.

## Printed cards and packaging

The quick-start card in [`docs/quick-start-card.md`](docs/quick-start-card.md) is headed "Your Sinko box" **only** for units
that may be sold as Sinko (the maintainer's own and those of the resellers named on the project website). If you sell your
own flashed units, put your own product name on the card and say "based on Sinko", as the rule above asks. The licence
sentence on the card stays either way.

## Please do not

* Present a **modified** version, or a fork, as Sinko. Forks must use a different name and logo and say that they are
  based on Sinko.
* Imply that the project endorses, certifies or supports your product, or suggest that a product is safe or
  child-proof because it uses Sinko. Sinko is a DNS filter and says so (see "What it cannot do" in the README).
* Use "Pi-hole", its logo, or a confusable name in your product name. Pi-hole is a registered trademark of Pi-hole LLC
  and Sinko is independent software that works with it ([Pi-hole's rules](https://pi-hole.net/trademark-rules-and-brand-guidelines/)).
